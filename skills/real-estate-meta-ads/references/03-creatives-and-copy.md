# 03. Creatives and copy — honest, specific, high-ticket

Buying property is a big, rational decision. Aggressive hooks bring the curious, not buyers, and may break consumer-protection and advertising rules in your jurisdiction.

## Principles

1. **Specific beats generic:** "2 bedrooms, 58 m², 4 minutes from X station, delivery 06/2028" beats "the apartment of your dreams".
2. **One ad, one promise, one next step** (see the property / talk on WhatsApp / book a visit).
3. **Real proof:** photos/video of the site and construction, floor plan, delivery date, developer's name. No testimonial that does not exist; no "50,000 happy clients".
4. **Label illustrations:** renders carry a readable "illustrative image" notice. **Never** present an AI-generated property image as real.
5. **No artificial urgency.** Scarcity only if true and verifiable (e.g. "3 two-bedroom units left" — checked against stock that day).
6. **Price:** only if it exists and is current. "From $X" only with the real unit at that price. Payment conditions in readable type.

## Formats that work for property

- **Short video (0–60 s)** with captions (people watch muted): the first 3 s show the property/view, not the logo. Structure: 0–3 s strong image → 3–20 s what and where → 20–45 s differentiator/proof → close with call to action.
- Carousel: one room/differentiator per card, last card = call to action.
- Single image: best photo + one line of fact.
- Vertical (9:16) for Stories/Reels; check the safe area at the edges.

## Test grid

3 concepts × 2 hooks × 2 copies. Change **one** element at a time. Ad name: `<angle>_<format>_v<n>` (e.g. `view_reel_v2`).
Angles for property: location/commute · real value for money · floor plan/size · easier financing (only if real) · security/amenities · investment/rental income (careful with return promises: never guarantee).

## Copy structure (no manipulation)

- **Line 1:** a fact or concrete benefit (visible before "see more").
- **Body:** 2–3 lines: what it is, where, real conditions.
- **Call to action:** verb + result ("Book a visit", "Get the floor plan on WhatsApp").
- Avoid "poke the pain" formulas ("still paying rent?"). If you compare renting vs. buying, use real numbers and cite the source.

## Forbidden (check before sending for approval)

- "Last units", "today only", fake countdowns, "guaranteed", "no risk", guaranteed returns.
- Before/after, bold claims, invented testimonials, generic social proof.
- Price/size/delivery date different from the contract or official specification.
- Pre-sales of a new development before the registration/permit your jurisdiction requires.
- Copy rewritten automatically in bulk without legal review where your local rules require it.
