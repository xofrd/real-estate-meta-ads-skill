## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-15

### Added
- First public version of the skill in English (`skills/real-estate-meta-ads/`) and Brazilian Portuguese (`skills/real-estate-meta-ads-pt-br/`).
- Nine reference files: campaign structure and intent ladder; higher-intent instant forms and click-to-WhatsApp; honest creatives and copy; tracking to the CRM with lead stages (no-response → pre-qualified → qualified) and Conversion Leads; scored account audit; owner-readable reports; Housing special ad category by country and generic local-rules checklist; Confirm → Execute → Verify change protocol with access-security rules; property catalog (`home_listings`) for Facebook/Instagram and WhatsApp limits.
- README (EN and pt-BR), CONTRIBUTING, CODE_OF_CONDUCT, SECURITY, issue and pull request templates, MIT license.

### Known open questions
- Whether Conversion Leads accepts a negative ("bad lead") event for training.
- Whether Ads Manager forces the Housing category on home-listings catalog campaigns outside the US, Canada and Europe.
- Whether unofficial WhatsApp integrations can send conversation conversions back to Meta.
- All Meta policy statements are as read on 2026-11-15 and must be re-checked.
